# Top GitHub repos: 2026-11-18

## TL;DR
- **Agent skills and harnesses still dominate.** Eight of the 15 picks exist to extend, evaluate or drive coding agents: last30days-skill, ECC, superpowers, CLI-Anything, stitch-skills, cursor/plugins, SkillEvaluator, and paper-orchestra (multi-agent paper writing).
- **Agent skills now have quality tooling.** NVIDIA's `SkillEvaluator` (new on Trending) adds quality gates and CI templates for testing skills, a sign the category is maturing.
- **Brand-new breakouts.** `deepseek-ai/chat-demo` (4,891 stars in about 6 days) and `erkamyaman/rust-anything` (3,684 stars in about 2 days) lead the fresh repos. `abyssal-garden` (a one-file WebGPU demo) and `huggingface/pybot` (a rule-based chatbot with no model weights) round out the week's novelty.
- **Old infrastructure is still in demand.** Svelte (+692), Arcane (+547) and n8n (+325) gained stars alongside the AI projects.
- **Caution.** Several new repos look star-inflated (for example `Wireless-Device-Battery-Monitor`, 4,139 stars with 2,545 open issues, was left out for that reason). Treat stars as attention, not quality.

**Method notes.** "Stars today" and total stars for the first 11 repos come from the GitHub Trending daily page fetched 2026-11-18. For picks 5, 6, 14 and 15, totals, forks, issues, license and dates come from the GitHub search API (created in the last week, sorted by stars), fetched the same day, and "stars today" is unknown because they were not on Trending. I did not read every README, and I did not retrieve per-repo release data, contributor counts or HN/Reddit threads, so those are "unknown". Design notes are high level, from descriptions only. Repeat markers compare with the 2026-11-17 report, which I read; earlier reports were not checked. Language/license are "unknown" where I did not retrieve them.
